The scenario separates two successful researcher reports across observed relay cycles. It requires no early wake, one final batch wake to the original active parent, both answers read before acknowledgement, and no duplicate wake after acknowledgement. Native receipts distinguish a queued notification from an active-wait return.

I rejected simultaneous completions because they cannot expose a wake after only the first report. I rejected sleep-only timing because it does not prove the relay processed the partial batch. I rejected manually seeded queue records and manual parent messages because they bypass the production owners of completion and delivery. Lost-child behavior stays outside this successful-report scenario.

This is an acceptance design reviewed against ADR 0067 and relay.py, not an executed product test. No product code changed and no agents were dispatched.
