# Write for Where It Is Read

A person may read the result in this conversation or on a web page. A model may start with an empty context. A shell may show one truncated line.

**Pattern:** before you write it, name who will pick it up, what they are trying to do at that moment, and what they can and cannot see from there. Then write for that person, agent or program.

**The test:** a thing that only makes sense from where you sit has not been built yet.

Distinct from the [technical-writing](../technical-writing/SKILL.md) skill, which shapes the sentences. This decides, before any sentence, whom they are for and what that reader can see.
