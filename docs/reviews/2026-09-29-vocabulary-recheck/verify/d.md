# Verify group d: the cross-context lists and yesterday's leftovers

Sources merged: the `## One word, several concepts` and `## Concepts with no entry` sections of all twelve files under `candidates/`. Every sense, count and line below comes from a fresh grep of `mmw-v2/` (skill text, scripts, merge-notes, board), `docs/contexts/`, `AGENTS.md` and `CONTEXT-MAP.md`; the reviewers' own counts were not reused. Proposals owned by `verify/a.md`, `b.md` and `c.md` are not re-decided; where one bears on a word here, its row number is cited.

The test applied in part 1: a qualified compound (`test layer`, `wake queue`, `release finding`) tells its sense apart, as the 2026-09-28 round accepted for "hook" (host hook, build hook, git hook). A conflict is a bare use of the word, or a definition, in a second sense, where an agent could read one sense for the other. The sense that keeps the bare word is chosen by `SKILL-SET-RULES.md` `### Vocabulary`: an upstream skill's own term keeps upstream's sense; otherwise the established term of the field; an identifier is its concept's one name.

Outcomes in part 1: **CHANGE** (real conflict; text or glossary changes as stated), **DEFINITION-ONLY** (only glossary wording changes), **TOLD APART** (no change), **OWNED** (a proposal of group a, b or c decides it; cross-context picture only), **OWNER** (a product surface would change).
