## Same concept, several names

- **Blocking between tickets**: "blocking link" (docs/agents/issue-tracker.md, night.md/tickets.md CONTEXT), "native issue dependencies" (docs/agents/issue-tracker.md), and "blocking edges" (to-tickets/SKILL.md step 5 heading and body) all name the same tracker-native dependency.
- **"Made a ticket" outcome**: night.md's route entry says a `finding` child leaves the closing pass as "fixed, stale, or made a ticket"; triage/references/pipeline-issues.md and to-tickets' merge-notes give the literal command keyword `became-ticket` for that same outcome.
- **Out of Scope content**: to-spec's spec section `## Out of Scope` and wayfinder's map section `## Out of scope` are explicitly the same content ("The map's Out of scope carries into the spec's Out of Scope unchanged," to-spec/SKILL.md step 1), just differently capitalized headings in two documents.
- **Worker grade**: the persisted ticket label (`junior-worker`/`senior-worker`, tickets.md's "worker grade") and to-tickets/SKILL.md step 6's quiz field "**Worker**: `junior` or `senior`" name the same fact at two different moments (proposal vs. published label).
- **Read-back**: tickets.md's "publish" entry folds in "the read-back step" as a clause; to-tickets/SKILL.md step 8 calls this the same thing by title, "Read every ticket back," with no separate CONTEXT.md entry of its own.
- **Blocked by**: used as the ready-for-human ticket's own textual section (tickets.md's dedicated "Blocked by" entry) and, loosely, in to-tickets/SKILL.md step 5 and references/cutting-interface-tickets.md for an agent ticket's native blocking relationship, which otherwise has no ticket-body heading at all.

## Same name, several concepts

- **claim**: in the landing pipeline (tickets.md, docs/agents/issue-tracker.md), claiming a ticket means setting its assignee and posting a `ticket.claimed` event that begins a hold. In wayfinder (SKILL.md, docs/agents/issue-tracker.md's Wayfinding operations), claiming a decision ticket means only assigning it to oneself before work; no event, no hold.
- **frontier**: tickets.md itself already disambiguates this between the night's frontier (`status.py`, tickets `advance` may start now) and "wayfinder's frontier, a map's open, unblocked, unclaimed children" — both names are used constantly across this group's own skills (to-tickets, wayfinder, triage), so the collision is live in the group's text, not just noted in passing.
- **unblocked**: for the night's frontier, a ticket unblocks its dependents only once it has *landed* (merged to the base branch via `ticket.landed`). For wayfinder's frontier, a decision ticket unblocks its dependents once it is merely *closed* (comment + close, no landing concept applies to a decision ticket).
