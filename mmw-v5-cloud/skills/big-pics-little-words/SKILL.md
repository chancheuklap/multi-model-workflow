Before shaping the artifact, use **principle-write-for-where-it-is-read**, **principle-everything-at-hand**, and **principle-anchor-every-reference**.

When describing supplied files or an existing system, use **principle-read-before-you-conclude** before deciding what the artifact says.

When working from approved wording, decisions, or designs, use **principle-baseline-is-the-contract** before changing their presentation.

Before interpreting or presenting measured results or numerical comparisons, use **principle-explain-the-number** for the numbers.

Use **html** to help me understand what we're discussing. Show me with big pictures and the words needed to understand them without withholding key details.
Use your own taste, avoid lazy design, integrate real-world pictures/icons/visuals as needed but only selectively when it makes sense.
When you do use prose/detail: Be clear and avoid jargon. Explain things as if the viewer is new here.
