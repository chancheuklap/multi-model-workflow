路还看不清时：Chart a map、Resolve a map ticket 和 prototype

一块工作大到一个会话装不下、从这里到终点的路又看不清时，先不写 spec，先画地图。Chart a map 和你定终点、把路上的问题建成 tracker 上的决定票；之后每个新会话跑 Resolve a map ticket，只解决一张；地图清了，交给 Write a spec。这一课写 mmw-rebuild 分支上这两份 playbook、管地图格式的 wayfinder 技能、原型票要用的 prototype 技能现在的样子，和 v2 的文字各去了哪。两份 playbook 的全文在页里，构建时从仓库读出。最后几节写设计哪里错过、怎样证明的、还没做的。

1　地图在 tracker 上是什么

地图只做决定，不做实现：每张票是一个问题，答案是一个决定，地图清了就是再没有要定的事。票有四种：research 查一个事实，没人在场也能做；prototype 做一个粗的东西让你看了说；grilling 是对话，最常见；task 是做出决定之前必须先做完的事，比如注册一个服务才能判断它的接口。prototype 和 grilling 要你在场，agent 不替你答；task 能自己做的 agent 自己做，做不了才给你一份清单。这些都是 wayfinder 技能写的。地图、子票、先后和前沿查询在 tracker 上怎样表示，技能指向仓库的 docs/agents/issue-tracker.md 的 ## Wayfinding operations；那个文件不在，就说仓库还没接入，停下，不像 v2 那样退回本地 Markdown。技能里不再有「怎样画」「怎样解决」两节，那两节成了两份 playbook。

什么时候用：一个会话装得下的、决定都已经定了的，直接 Write a spec；装不下、路又看不清的，先画地图。归纳pstack 没有对应的东西。它的 multi-phase-plan.md 在一个会话里用原型定掉开放问题，再写成每个 PR 一节的计划，交给每个 PR 一个 owner 执行；它没有跨会话留在 tracker 上的决定和雾，对应的是 MMW 的 Write a spec 加 Cut tickets，所以地图取 v2 和 mattpocock 的。mattpocock 的 wayfinder 在 v2 拉进来之后没有改过，两份的差别全是 v2 自己的改动。

### Chart a map

**You own one map: its destination named with the owner, the map issue on the tracker, the decision tickets that can be stated now, and the answers of its research tickets; researcher sessions read for those, and no other ticket is resolved here.** A loose idea has arrived, too big for one session, and the way from here to its destination is not visible yet. Charting finds the questions on that way, not their answers: every ticket but a research one is resolved later, one per session, with Resolve a map ticket. What a map, a ticket, the fog and the out of scope are is the `wayfinder` skill; read it before step 1. Distinct from Write a spec, which starts from decisions already made, and from Resolve a map ticket, which works a map that exists.

1. **Name the destination.** Read the `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills' `SKILL.md` and run this round with the owner as both describe, to pin down what the map is finding its way to: the spec, the decision, or the change. The destination fixes the scope, so it is settled first.
   Done when the owner has agreed to one or two lines that say what reaching the end of the map looks like.
2. **Map the frontier.** Grill again, **breadth-first**: fan out across the whole space rather than deep on one thread, surfacing the open decisions and the first steps takeable now. When this surfaces no fog (the way is already clear, and the whole journey fits one session), no map is needed: tell the owner, name Write a spec as the route when the decisions are made, and ask how they want to proceed; this playbook ends here.
   Done when you hold the open decisions, each either sharp enough to be a ticket or written down as fog, and what the owner ruled out of scope.
3. **Create the map.** Write the map body in the shape of the `wayfinder` skill's `### The map body`: **Destination** and **Notes** filled in, the effort's directory name in **Notes**, **Decisions so far** empty, the fog sketched into **Not yet specified**, what the owner ruled out in **Out of scope**. Publish the map as one issue with the labels `wayfinder:map` and `mmw:map`, as the repository's `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` `## Wayfinding operations` says.
   Done when the map is on the tracker with both labels, and you hold its number.
4. **Create the tickets you can state now.** Create each as a child issue of the map, its body the `## Question` the `wayfinder` skill's `### Tickets` gives and its `wayfinder:<type>` label; then wire the blocking edges in a **second pass** (issues need ids before they can reference each other). Everything you cannot yet state sharply stays in **Not yet specified**. A destination with a UI, or one that remakes an existing product, adds the tickets the `mmw-mode` skill's `references/interface-and-remake.md` names.
   Done when every ticket you could state is a child of the map with its type label, and every blocking edge between them is wired.
5. **Answer the research tickets.** For each `research` ticket just created, write a brief from the `mmw-mode` skill's `references/map-research-brief.md`, with the ticket's question, the map's destination, and the note's path where the repository keeps research notes. Start them all in one `dispatch.sh brief researcher <file>...` call of the `dispatch` skill, then end your turn. On the wake `brief <batch> done`, follow the `dispatch` skill's `## On waking`. For each answer: save it at its note path, commit it on the base branch and push; post its `## Answer` and the note's path as the ticket's resolution comment and close the ticket; append its context pointer to the map's **Decisions so far**, one at a time, reading the map body fresh before each edit. Then `dispatch.sh brief close <batch>`.
   Done when every research ticket is closed with its note pushed and its pointer on the map, or the map has none.

**Reply:** the map's title and link; its destination; the tickets on the frontier now and the ones blocked behind them, by name; what is still fog and what was ruled out of scope; the research answers in one line each. The next move is a fresh session running Resolve a map ticket on this map.

### Resolve a map ticket

**You own one decision ticket of a map: claimed first, resolved by its type, recorded on the map, and the fog its answer clears turned into tickets.** One ticket per session: each ticket is sized to one session, and the map, not this session, is what the next session reads. The exception is research tickets, which a researcher session answers. What a map, a ticket, the fog and the out of scope are is the `wayfinder` skill; read it before step 1. Other sessions may be working other tickets of the same map at the same time, so read the map body fresh before every edit of it. Distinct from Chart a map, which creates the map, and from Work a ticket, which builds a ticket of a spec.

1. **Load the map.** Read the map body: the low-resolution view, not every ticket body. Read the `SKILL.md` of each skill its **Notes** names.
   Done when you know the destination, the decisions so far and the fog.
2. **Choose the ticket and claim it.** Take the ticket the owner named; otherwise the first ticket of the frontier query in the repository's `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` `## Wayfinding operations`. Claim it before any other work: `gh issue edit <n> --add-assignee @me`.
   Done when the ticket carries your assignee.
3. **Resolve it by its type,** as the `wayfinder` skill's `## Ticket Types` says, unless its body opens with a line naming a playbook (a map's design ticket or alignment ticket): then run that playbook, whatever the type. Zoom as needed: fetch the full body of any related or closed ticket. For a `grilling` ticket, read the `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills' `SKILL.md` and grill the owner; for a `prototype` ticket, read the `prototype` skill's `SKILL.md` and follow it, and the owner reacts to what it builds; for a `task` ticket, do the work, or hand the owner a precise checklist for what only they can do. A HITL ticket resolves only through the owner's own answers; never answer for them. For a `research` ticket, brief one researcher with `dispatch.sh brief researcher <file>`, the brief written from the `mmw-mode` skill's `references/map-research-brief.md`, and save, record and close it as Chart a map step 5 says when the wake comes; a research ticket does not count as this session's one ticket.
   Done when the ticket has its answer: the decision, the facts, or the work done.
4. **Record the resolution.** Post the answer as the ticket's resolution comment, close it, and append a context pointer (the ticket's name, linked, and a one-line gist) to the map's **Decisions so far**. A prototype's resolution names its leaf directory; a decision the owner made says so.
   Done when the ticket is closed with its resolution comment and its pointer is on the map.
5. **Move the frontier.** Create the tickets the answer has made statable, as child issues with their type labels, then wire their blocking edges; when the answer gives the destination a UI, or makes it a remake, and the map lacks the tickets the `mmw-mode` skill's `references/interface-and-remake.md` names, add them as it says; clear each patch of fog that graduated from **Not yet specified**. A ticket the answer shows to sit beyond the destination is ruled out of scope as the `wayfinder` skill's `## Out of scope` says, not resolved. A ticket the decision invalidated is updated or closed with a comment saying why.
   Done when the map's tickets, fog and out-of-scope lines all agree with the answer.
6. **Check whether the map is clear,** as the `wayfinder` skill's `## A clear map` says. When it is, the next move is a fresh session running Write a spec with this map as its reference; leave the map open.
   Done when you know whether the map is clear.

**Reply:** the ticket's name and its answer; the tickets it opened, closed or ruled out of scope, by name; the frontier now; and, when the map is clear, that the next move is Write a spec on this map in a fresh session.

4　研究票交给 researcher 会话

v2 派的是主机自己的通用子代理，子代理自己存文件、贴评论、关票。v3 里研究已经是一个会话角色：how、why 用 dispatch.sh brief researcher 开，它能在 ~/.mmw/models.json 给的模型上跑，答完由中继叫醒开它的会话（第 6 课第 9 节）。地图的研究票用同一个角色，roles.json 里 researcher 一行的 sent_by 加了两份地图 playbook，brief 加了 mmw-mode/references/map-research-brief.md。

研究员不碰仓库和 tracker，只把答案写成一个文件交回。原因有两个。第一，v2 的合并记录写着：研究笔记留在一条没推送的本机分支上，spec 引用它、worker 读它时打不开（agentflow #592），它的办法是存进仓库放研究笔记的地方。归纳存进去还不够，要提交并推送，别的会话和 worker 才读得到；交给一个会话做，提交只有一处。第二，归纳几个研究员同时改地图正文会互相覆盖，v2 已经让画图的会话一条一条加指针，现在连评论和关票也一起收过来。

5　地图清了以后

wayfinder 的 ## A clear map：没有一张决定票还开着，Not yet specified 是空的。清了的地图不关，它是这块工作的决定索引。下一步是新会话跑 Write a spec，以地图为引用。第 9 课时 Write a spec 里读地图的一支没有搬，这一课搬回来了：

  • 第 1 步：读地图正文，走一遍 Decisions so far，读每张关了的票的 resolution comment，链到的原型或研究文件读到结论；地图的 Out of scope 原样进 spec 的 Out of Scope。
  • 分成几份 spec 时，分法写回地图的 ## Specs 一节；这一次只写第一份；地图已有 ## Specs 时不再判断，写第一个还没有链接的。
  • 第 4 步：发布时加 --map <map>，verify-ticket.py 把 spec 挂成地图的子 issue，并确认挂上了，没挂上就退出 1。
  • 模板的 Sources 加回 Wayfinder map 一行；triage 的例外加回「地图、spec 和决定票都不进 triage」。

6　prototype：搬了什么、没搬什么

原型票要读 prototype。v2 的它有三支：LOGIC 回答「这套状态和逻辑对不对」，做一个你能点的单页；EXP 回答「到底该怎样实现」，做一个最小的能跑的实验，每跑一次写一页证据；UI 回答「应该长什么样」，做几种截然不同的界面让你挑，赢的那种交给 Claude Design。这一课先搬了 LOGIC 和 EXP；UI 那一支在第 12 课和 design-pages 一起回来。v2 要 effort 目录名在 prototypes/ 和 docs/specs/（screen contract 所在）下相同；地图 Notes 一行和 prototype 的规则 1 都这样写，docs/specs/ 也是第 12 课补回的。

和 pstack 的 prototype.md 对读，留 v2 的。pstack 的原型用完就扔；v2 的原型留在仓库 prototypes/<effort>/<issue>/，旁边的 README.md 写问题和结论。v2 合并记录写的理由是：spec 引用这份结论、票照抄它的精确值、worker 夜里照它施工，读的人都不在那个会话里，结论只写一句「见某处讨论」时读不到决定本身。

7　界面和重做产品

v2 的 wayfinder/references/interface-and-remake.md（572 词）这一课没搬，第 12 课把它放进 mmw-mode/references/，由 Chart a map 第 4 步和 Resolve a map ticket 第 5 步读。它管两种终点要多开的票：有界面的，要一张 design ticket（在 Claude Design 里画页）和一张 alignment ticket（写 screen contract，最后关）；重做一个已有产品的，要一张挑选清单（旧产品留哪些、删哪些），产品已上线、有付费客户时再加一张票定新旧界面怎样并存、怎样切换。这些都要 design-pages 和 write-screen-contract，两者也在第 12 课。

11　自测

1．你说「做一个记账 App，从零开始」，其中用什么数据库、要不要多币种都还没想。会话该跑什么？

一个会话装不下，路也看不清（图 1）。先 Chart a map：定终点，把这些没定的问题建成决定票。决定都定了，才轮到 Write a spec。

2．地图上有一张 grilling 票「多币种要不要第一版就做」，你今天不在。会话该怎么做？

grilling 票要人在场，agent 不替你答（wayfinder 的 ## Ticket Types）。一个会话只解决一张，也不会去做别的票；它停在这里，等你。

3．画图时开了三个 researcher。谁把研究笔记提交进仓库、在票上贴答案？

研究员只交一个文件（图 4）。画图的会话被叫醒后一张一张存、提交推送、评论、关票、加指针，这样只有它写仓库和地图。

4．地图分成两份 spec，第一份已用 --map 发布，还开着；决定票都关了，雾也空了。地图清了吗？

只数决定票（## A clear map），spec 是子 issue 但不算。清了的地图不关；Write a spec 读地图的 ## Specs，写第一个还没有链接的那份。

5．一张 prototype 票问「首页应该长什么样」。现在会怎样？
