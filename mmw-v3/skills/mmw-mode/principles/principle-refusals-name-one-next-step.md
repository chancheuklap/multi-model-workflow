# Refusals name one next step

A refusal names one fact it checked, says why, and gives one next step. When you are refused, do that step and do not improvise another.

**Why:** A refusal gives one way out, not a list: an agent given options invents a sixth.

**Pattern:**
- **Look at the fact.** The fact is a pid, a path, a count or a ticket number, something you can go and look at yourself.
