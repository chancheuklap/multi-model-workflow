name: research
description: Run only when the user, mmw-mode or another skill names it; do not invoke it on your own.

Investigate a question against high-trust primary sources and capture the findings as a Markdown file in the repo. A researcher session does it: the `dispatch` skill's `dispatch.sh research <brief file>` starts one, with `Use the research skill.` and the brief as its whole prompt. If that is how you were started, read [Researching](references/researching.md).
