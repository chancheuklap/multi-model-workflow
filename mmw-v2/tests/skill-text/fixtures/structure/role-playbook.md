### Work a ticket

**You own the plan. Keep its criteria explicit.**

**Where you are.** Run `dispatch.sh where <n>` and go to its step.

#### Steps

1. **Read the evidence.** Use the `example` skill.
   Done when the evidence is recorded.
2. **Judge the result.** (judgement) Compare the evidence with the criterion.
   Done when the criterion is decided.

#### While checking

Keep each criterion explicit.

**Reply:** the recorded decision.
