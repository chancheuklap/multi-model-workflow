# A second reader judges

The judgement of a piece of work goes to a reader who did not write it, in another context or another session.

**Why:** The value is a session that has not spent the last hour convincing itself.

**Pattern:**
- **Leave the question open.** A second opinion you steered is your own opinion in another voice.
