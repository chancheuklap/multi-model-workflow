# The baseline is a contract

A baseline is a decision someone already paid for: a user's answer, a prototype that won, a page they signed off. It is a contract, not a reference.

**Why:** Rewriting it from memory, or improving it, reopens that decision where nobody who made it can see.

**Pattern:**
- **Copy it, do not rewrite it.** Copy the values, wording, states and interface shapes of a baseline from it rather than writing them again from memory.
- **Reopen it in the open.** When a baseline does not hold, quote what does not hold, state what in it still holds and must be preserved, and put that where the people who made it can see.

**Stop:** Never quietly change a baseline, never quietly add around one.
