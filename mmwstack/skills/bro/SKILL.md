Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve.

Then restate your last message. I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that: give me a little bit of context, talk in the style of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, in the language the user writes in, and use the ubiquitous language from `GLOSSARY.md` (follow `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` to the right one if the repo has more than one). Stop using jargon and speak coherently. State it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another.

If what the user asks for after `/bro` is clearly one of these skills' jobs, skip both steps and follow that skill instead. A skill's own description settles a close call. When none clearly fits, do the two steps above.
